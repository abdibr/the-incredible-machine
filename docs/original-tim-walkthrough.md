# The Incredible Machine — original 87 levels & walkthrough reference

> Distilled from [The Sierra Help Pages walkthrough](https://sierrahelp.com/Walkthroughs/TheIncredibleMachineWalkthrough.html),
> which solves all 87 puzzles of the original *The Incredible Machine* (1993).
> Descriptions below are condensed summaries, not the full verbatim solutions — see
> the source page for the blow-by-blow instructions and alternate solutions.

## Notes from the source

- **Passwords:** The source skips listing passwords for levels 2–86 and notes only
  that `GULF` is the password for level 87 — entering it unlocks access to *any* of
  the game's puzzles.
- **Size convention:** "Size 1" is the smallest (shortest) of a trampoline or
  conveyor belt; "size 2" one step larger, and so on up to the maximum — size 4
  for an incline, size 5 for a conveyor belt.
- **The cast:** recurring goal characters — Mort the Mouse (get him home / trap
  him / free him), Pokey the Cat (trap / free / chase), Bob the Fish (save /
  lure), Kelly the Monkey (exercise via her bicycle).
- Many puzzles can be solved several different ways; the walkthrough's answers are
  springboards, not the easiest or most elegant builds.

## Levels 1–30 (mostly tutorial)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Put the Ball in the Hoop | Tutorial: mouse motors & belts. Each falling bowling ball trips a mouse motor driving the next belt up; the basketball rides its belt into the hoop. |
| 2 | Mirror Images | Tutorial: build a mirror image of the left-side machine on the right — remember to reverse the mouse motor or the bowling ball flies off. |
| 3 | Bellows and Balloons | Tutorial: bellows. Blow the top balloon into the rotating gear and the bottom balloon into the scissors. |
| 4 | Flip, Flip, Flip | Tutorial: seesaws. Place a seesaw under each cannonball so each flipped ball flips the next in line. |
| 5 | Punch out | Tutorial: boxing gloves & seesaws. Seesaws flip cannonballs up; gloves knock the bowling balls and baseball down their channels home. |
| 6 | Bouncing over to Mort | Tutorial: trampolines. Lower trampoline = higher bounce; place three so the basketball bounces across and frees Mort from his cage. |
| 7 | Jack Says, "Hi Bob" | Tutorial: jack-in-the-boxes. The flipped cannonball trips a second mouse motor that pops the jack under Bob the Fish. |
| 8 | Tilting at Windmills | Tutorial: windmills. Three bellows→windmill→belt/gear chains; each ball falls through a hole to power the next leg until the balloon meets the gear. |
| 9 | Lower All the Buckets | Tutorial: scissors. Cannonball cuts the left rope; the baseball bounces off a trampoline into the right-hand scissors. |
| 10 | Bang, Bang, Bang | Tutorial: guns & ropes. Ropes from a seesaw and a pail, run through pulleys, pull the triggers of two guns. |
| 11 | Like a Hurricane | Tutorial: switches & fans. Baseball bounces off a trampoline to flip a switch, whose fan blows the balloon into the next switch, cascading on. |
| 12 | Generators and Motors | Tutorial: generators & motors. Windmill-powered generator drives motor + belt; a mouse motor powers a second generator; the fan blows the tennis ball into the pipe hole. |
| 13 | Putting the Gears in Motion | Tutorial: gears & solar panels. Flashlight-fed solar panels plus a pail-on-bulb light a chain of outlet motors that drive belts and the gears. |
| 14 | Lighting a Fuse | Tutorial: cannons & fuses. Follow the worked example — flashlight + magnifying glass light each cannon's fuse. |
| 15 | Boom, Boom, Bang | Tutorial: dynamite. Rope the pail to a lightbulb; the flying basketball triggers a flashlight/magnifier that ignites the dynamite. |
| 16 | Blastoff | Tutorial: rockets. Bowling ball lights the second rocket's fuse; the rising rocket trips a seesaw that raises a lightbulb to light the candle. |
| 17 | Doing Some Blasting | Tutorial: dynamite plungers. Seesaw + rope fires the gun via its trigger; dynamite placed on the three left shelves gets blasted. |
| 18 | Sending Mort the Mouse Home | Tutorial: cats & mice. Bowling ball off a trampoline smashes Bob's bowl; mouse motor + belt sends Mort home; Pokey chases him into his hole. |
| 19 | Monkey Business | Tutorial: monkeys & bicycles. A piece of cake — tie a rope from the cage to the left monkey's shade. |
| 20 | Bridging the Gap | Tutorial: inclines. A size-2 incline fills the middle gap; seesaws + rope over pulleys bridge the rest of the track. |
| 21 | Climbing a Hill | Like puzzle 1: fill each gap with a connected mouse motor and size-1 belt — and say "So Long" to Pokey. |
| 22 | Turn, Turn, Turn... Pop, Pop, Pop | The tutorial ends — connect all the gears: monkeys' shades, meshed gears, belts and mouse motor so the drive chain pops the balloons. |
| 23 | Trick Shooting | Three guns, each tied to a seesaw by rope, shoot the top, middle and bottom baseballs into the container (don't hit dead-center or they bounce off the wall). |
| 24 | Save the Balloons | Tie balloons 2 and 4 together over two pulleys as a counterweight pair so neither floats too high. |
| 25 | Pop Two Balloons | A fresh balloon roped over a pulley lifts and drags the rightmost balloon into the gear, then swings into the mouse motor. |
| 26 | Dropping the Ball | Gun + cannonball timing: seesaw/lightbulb fires the cannon to clear a pail; conveyor-belt "steps" slow the bowling ball so the second seesaw fires the gun, sending it into the cave. |
| 27 | Popping Balloons on the Moon | Pop balloon 1 with the left gear (incline onto trampoline), balloon 2 with the scissors, balloon 3 with a seesaw-triggered gun. |
| 28 | A Baseball in Every Pot | All you need are six seesaws — three under the left baseballs, three mirrored under the right; the bowling ball cascades them all home. |
| 29 | Weighing the Situation | Counterweighted pails on pulleys plus carefully timed tennis/cannonball drops open a window for the baseball to roll underneath. |
| 30 | Pop All the Balloons | Bowling ball trips Pokey → seesaws → monkey pedals the gears, driving conveyor belts that carry the left balloon along an incline into the gears; the right balloon never has a chance. |

## Levels 31–60

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 31 | Put Away the Basketballs | A size-4 belt (mouse motor powered) plus an incline routes all three basketballs into the container; optional seesaw lid keeps them in. |
| 32 | Five Gun Salute | Baseball + three pairs of scissors free several balloons; each freed balloon then trips a seesaw-tied gun for a five-gun salute. |
| 33 | A Farewell to Balloons | Classic Rube Goldberg chain working counter-clockwise — fan, tennis ball, flashlight, candle, generators/gears/motors, jack-in-the-box — ending with a flaming rocket bursting the balloon. |
| 34 | Pop Goes the Weasel | Mouse motor drives the jack-in-the-box through two pairs of meshed gears; a lightbulb-powered fan blows the basketball into the seesaw that drives the motor's seesaw. |
| 35 | Wheeeeeee! | Line up a set of three trampolines plus inclines so the bowling ball bounces its way into the pipe basket. |
| 36 | Pokey and Bob Shoot it out | A timing puzzle: drop the second bowling ball onto scissors to cut the pail rope, then tweak until the bouncing pails/balls block the two shooters' bullets. |
| 37 | Mouse in the House | Fan-blow Mort left onto an incline; a motor/generator/fan chain then blows him into his house. |
| 38 | Inside the Walls | Baseball powers bellows→windmill→belt under the bowling ball; trampolines fill the piping gaps and bounce the ball into the brick basket. |
| 39 | Trap Pokey the Cat | Control the cannonball by placing the cannon above the brick wall (it bounces back); bowling ball herds Pokey under the cage just as the scissors drop it. |
| 40 | Going in the Hole | Seesaw rolls the top basketball left; a fan next to a switch-operated outlet (turned on by the balloon) blows both balls right into the hole. |
| 41 | Help Pokey Get Home | Fishbowl and mouse "lures" placed at the gaps and edges walk Pokey level by level into his house. |
| 42 | Ring of Fire | Light one stick of dynamite: incline-rolled tennis ball hits the flashlight, magnifier ignites the second dynamite — the explosion does the rest. |
| 43 | Lower the Bucket | A lit candle (bulb from a seesaw-pulled rope) is shot rightward along the belts by the gear/motor chain, blowing out the balloon and lowering the bucket. |
| 44 | Play a Set | Dynamite projects the tennis ball right; the rocket doubles as scissors-cutter and fuse-lighter off a makeshift launch pad; a trampoline + gun-balanced seesaw elevates the ball. |
| 45 | You Gotta Smile | Tennis ball sends the balloon into a switched outlet; its motor spins the gear just below the right eye. |
| 46 | Save Bob the Fish | Dynamite blows out the bottom-right wall so the cannonball rolls harmlessly away; monkey/candle/belt chain completes the save. |
| 47 | Fetch a Pail | Meshed gear train and two belts transport the right pail across the screen; a second mouse motor re-routes it right along the bottom belt. |
| 48 | Exercise Kelly the Monkey | Cannonball relay (mouse motor, belts, trampoline, seesaw) ends in a lightning-lit rocket that strikes the seesaw tied to Kelly's shade. |
| 49 | Feeling a Little out of Sorts | Seven balls, several inclines and one seesaw deflect each ball left/right into its correct hole in sequence — a matter of time and positioning more than timing. |
| 50 | Pop, Pop, Pop, Pop, And... Pop | Conveyor belts under the balloons; a baseball lights the candle riding the left belt and trips the mouse motor that starts the pops. |
| 51 | Replacing Bob's Bowl | Size-3 belt + mouse motor carry the cannonball; paired inclines guard the channels and two trampolines deliver it to Bob's bowl. |
| 52 | Trap Mort the Mouse | Tennis ball onto a switched outlet (fan), then the boxing glove and seesaw send the ball onto a trampoline beneath the scissors. |
| 53 | Red Alert! | A candle lit by seesaw/bulb rides a size-1 belt; the falling cannonball starts the mouse motor that carries the flame right into the fireworks. |
| 54 | Set off Fireworks | Chain reaction: flashlight-lit rocket crashes into Bob, luring Pokey toward Mort; Mort cuts the pail rope; pail hits the plunger, launching the tennis ball up a pipe to switch on the motor→generator→fan→jack chain; cannonball lights the last flashlight. |
| 55 | Punch the Bucket | Bowling ball, baseball and cannonball: incline + seesaw flip the baseball, which flips the cannonball into the boxing glove. |
| 56 | Happy Second Birthday | Same idea as #55: cannonball bounces off a trampoline into a seesaw that deflects it into the boxing glove. |
| 57 | Let Mort out of the Box | Baseball off trampoline + glove onto a plunger blasts a hole in the box; the ball bounces onto a seesaw tied to Kelly inside, whose belt runs Mort to freedom. |
| 58 | Exercise All Four Mice | Symmetric build: two basketballs perpetually flip each other into central mouse motors driving belts, sending the tennis balls down the chutes to the outer motors — all four mice run simultaneously. |
| 59 | Lower the Boom | Baseball→seesaw→lightbulb fires the cannon; its ball snips the scissors-held rope, dropping the pail into the hole at bottom center. |
| 60 | Cat-a-pulting | Cannonball launched by seesaw lands in the bucket, raising the bucket blocking Pokey; the basketball bounces on into the boxing glove that knocks Pokey off the edge. |

## Levels 61–87

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 61 | Put Mort in Prison | Incline-led baseball cuts the cage rope; trampoline-deflected dynamite and a seesaw-tied gun blow up the wooden flooring; inclines delay the bowling ball. |
| 62 | Eliminate the Balloons | Size-4 incline staircase routes the baseball around Kelly onto the seesaw; roping it to Kelly's shade starts the pedaling that drives the belt with its dire message to the balloons. |
| 63 | Launch All the Rockets | Baseball and basketball each trip seesaws roped through pulleys to flashlight/magnifier pairs igniting the bottom-left and bottom-right rockets. |
| 64 | Fire the Cannon | Two mouse motor/belt combos whoosh the ball left then right; a third belt directs it down the wooden channel onto the flashlight. |
| 65 | Break Bob's Fishbowl | Boxing glove→mouse motor→generator→motor→gear chain; fans deflect the tennis ball onto the flashlight whose magnifier lights the dynamite. |
| 66 | Knock it off | Baseball onto a mouse-motor-driven belt to the other baseball; a gear bumps the floating balloon into the mouse motor. "Baseball in the side pocket." |
| 67 | Ten, Nine, Eight, Ignition Sequence Start... | The goal never says you must *ignite* the rocket: a seesaw flips it onto a trampoline — up, up and away. |
| 68 | Mort-trap | Gear+belt pair driven by the mouse motor; Pokey lands on a seesaw tied to the gun; dynamite under the cage blows the brick so the cage lands on Mort. |
| 69 | Shedding Some Light | The first seesaw's connected to the... first pulley — rope each of four lightbulbs through its pulley to the right seesaw so falling balls light them all. |
| 70 | Save the Bob Squad | The layout lies: just deflect the left cannonball with a seesaw and cut the lightbulb's rope with a baseball/scissors combo — then wait for TIM to notice the win. |
| 71 | The Wooden Shaft | Tennis ball→scissors frees a balloon that lifts a seesaw compressing the bellows; the blown windmill powers generator→motor→belt under the three baseballs. |
| 72 | Laying down a Bunt | Cannonball→seesaw→lightbulb powers the solar outlet, whose fan blows the baseball left; pipe pieces direct it into the bucket. |
| 73 | A Pirate's Life for Me | A dynamite blast (tennis ball-triggered flashlight/magnifier) frees the candle, which lands on the mouse-motor-driven belt lit by the flashlight and gets delivered to its appointed destination. |
| 74 | Chase Away the Mice | One cannonball does three jobs — flips the outlet/fan, bumps the mouse motor and gun, then rolls down the incline ramp onto the plunger, blowing the brick lip before exiting. |
| 75 | Blow up | Six seesaws under the bowling ball, each roped to a gun — every flip fires a gun and a stick of dynamite; the sixth flip also lights the candle that ends the stick hit by the boxing glove. |
| 76 | Balloons in Danger | The trick: a balloon popped by a bullet also stops that bullet. Place balloons to shield the third gun and its rope-cutter. |
| 77 | Getting the Balls Together | A basketball dropped above/beside the bowling ball nudges both onto the belt route; the bowling ball's seesaw fires a gun that explodes a path for the baseball. |
| 78 | Free Poor Pokey the Cat | Tie a balloon to the seesaw under the cannonball (bounced off a mouse motor by an incline): the balloon lifts the seesaw end, flipping the ball into the bucket that raises the cage. |
| 79 | Put the Balls into the Baskets | A balloon-tied seesaw arms the lower mouse motor/belt for the basketball; a meshed gear pair reverses the upper belt for the bowling ball into the taller basket. |
| 80 | Basketball on the Moon | A jack-in-the-box under the basketball (geared to Kelly's bicycle) takes the foul shot; dynamite under the cage is lit by baseball-triggered flashlight/magnifier. |
| 81 | Breaking down the Wall | Two seesaw/lightbulb circuits: one lights the dynamite breaking the wall (dynamite rides a trampoline level with the cannon muzzle), the other fires the cannon into the bucket. |
| 82 | Bounce, Bounce, Bounce Go the Balls | A trampoline ladder across the wells: an incline deflects the basketball left and five trampolines carry the balls into the pipe basket. |
| 83 | Light My Fire | A candle lit on the belts rides right to ignite the rocket, which then flies *sideways* along the pedestals lighting the candle row; the last falling candle lights the final two via seesaw/bulb. |
| 84 | Removing the Pattern | Two cannonballs drop into the two buckets; the right bucket hits the scissors at the same moment its ball lands — then watch the pattern unravel. |
| 85 | Put the Cage in the Hole | The dropped bucket fires the gun that shoots the cage off its base onto a belt network; the baseball cuts the rope at the scissors; belt/incline chain dumps the cage in the hole. |
| 86 | Save Mort from the Cats | Mort bumps a right-facing mouse motor that drives a size-4 belt above the far-right gear — and his problems are behind him. |
| 87 | Up, Up, and Away | Lots of red herrings: the lower baseball just needs to reach the flashlight — seesaw, rollers and a mouse-motor belt throw it down the hole onto the light. Password: `GULF`. |
